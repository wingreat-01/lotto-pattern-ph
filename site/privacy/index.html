Who we are

LottoPattern PH (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by prohubapps.com. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to lottopatternph@prohubapps.com.

What the app does

The app generates six-number combinations for Philippine lotto games using patterns such as Plus 2, Twin, Reversed, Plus 10, Plus 20, Plus 30 and Solo, and it shows past draw results with the patterns found in them.

  • Patterns describe past and generated numbers. They do not improve your chances of winning, and every draw is random.
  • The app does not sell lottery tickets, take bets, or handle prizes.
  • The app is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by PCSO.
  • Use it for entertainment and pattern study only. Please play responsibly.

Information we collect

No account needed

The app has no sign-in and no user accounts. We do not ask for, receive or store your name, email address or profile picture.

Purchase status

If you buy Lifetime Access (Remove Ads), Google Play records the purchase against your Google Play account. The app checks with Google Play to confirm you own it and remembers the result on your device so the ads stay off. We do not receive your payment card details.

Advertising information

The app shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. See Ads and purchases below.

Technical information

When the app contacts our servers or the services listed below, they receive your IP address and ordinary request details such as the app version and time. These appear in standard server logs.

What we do not collect

  • The number combinations you generate. They are created on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Payment card details. If you buy Lifetime Access through Google Play, Google processes the payment.

Apart from Google AdMob for ads, the app does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. The advertising ID is collected by AdMob, not stored by us.

Ads and purchases

Rewarded ads

The app is free to download and use. To generate a pattern, you watch a short rewarded video ad. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use your advertising ID and similar device information to show and measure ads, and may show ads based on your interests where this is allowed.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization on your Android device under Settings > Google > Ads (the path may vary by device). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Remove Ads (one-time purchase)

You can remove ads permanently with a one-time Lifetime Access purchase of ₱199 (the price shown in Google Play may vary slightly by country and tax). The purchase is made through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and handles refunds under Google Play’s refund policy. You can restore the purchase on a new device by signing in to the same Google Play account.

How we use it

  • To show rewarded ads and, if you buy Lifetime Access, to turn the ads off.
  • To confirm purchases made through Google Play, and to prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To show past draw results and fix problems with the app.
  • To answer your messages and meet our legal duties.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Google AdMob collects and uses certain device data directly to show and measure ads, as described in Ads and purchases. We share information with the other services above only so the app can work, when the law or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect the app, our users or others from fraud and abuse. If prohubapps.com is sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner, who must honor this policy.

How long we keep it

We do not keep personal profiles of our users. Ordinary server logs at the services above are kept for a short time and then deleted under their own policies. Data the app saves on your device stays there until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Your purchase record is held by Google Play under Google’s own policies.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), and similar laws where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete your information,
  • stop or limit how we use it, and
  • give you a copy of it.

Because we hold very little information that identifies you, we may not be able to find your data without extra details. Email lottopatternph@prohubapps.com and tell us what you need. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, because the app does not create one. To remove what the app stores:

  • Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. This removes everything saved on your device, including your saved pattern settings.
  • To reset ad data, change your advertising ID or opt out of personalization in Android under Settings > Google > Ads.
  • For purchases and refunds, use Google Play, since Google holds that record.
  • For anything else, email lottopatternph@prohubapps.com.

Security

We collect very little, and the services we use protect data in transit. Your generated numbers stay on your device. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection.

Age limit

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will help.

Where data is processed

Our service providers may process your information on servers outside the Philippines. By using the app you understand that your information may be handled in those places, under the protections these providers offer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your personal information, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies.
