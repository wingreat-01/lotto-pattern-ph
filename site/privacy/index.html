Who we are

LottoPattern PH (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by prohubapps.com. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to lottopatternph@prohubapps.com.

What the app does

The app generates six-number combinations for Philippine lotto games using patterns such as Plus 2, Twin, Reversed, Plus 10, Plus 20, Plus 30 and Solo, and it shows past draw results with the patterns found in them.

  • Patterns describe past and generated numbers. They do not improve your chances of winning, and every draw is random.
  • The app does not sell lottery tickets, take bets, or handle prizes.
  • The app is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by PCSO.
  • Use it for entertainment and pattern study only. Please play responsibly.

Information we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google. Google shares basic profile details with us, such as your email address, your name and your profile picture, along with a unique account ID. We never see your Google password. Sign-in and account records are handled for us by Supabase.

Access and usage status

To run the free trial and credits, we store:

  • when your trial started and when it ends,
  • how many generation credits you have left,
  • whether you have unlocked Lifetime Access, and
  • a random request ID created each time you generate numbers, so that a retry is never charged twice.

Technical information

When the app contacts our servers or the services listed below, they receive your IP address and ordinary request details such as the app version and time. These appear in standard server logs.

What we do not collect

  • The number combinations you generate. They are created on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising ID.
  • Payment card details. If you buy Lifetime Access through Google Play, Google processes the payment.

The app does not contain advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To give you your free trial, count your credits and unlock features you are entitled to.
  • To limit the free trial to one per eligible Google account, and to prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To show past draw results and fix problems with the app.
  • To answer your messages and meet our legal duties.

Services we rely on

The app works through the services below. Each one handles information under its own privacy policy.

Google Sign-in with your Google account, and distribution of the app through Google Play. Your sign-in activity. We receive your email, name, picture and account ID from Google.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the services above so the app can work, when the law or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect the app, our users or others from fraud and abuse. If prohubapps.com is sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner, who must honor this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and access records for as long as your account is active. After you delete your account, we remove your personal information. We may keep a minimal record needed to prevent repeated free trials, and anything we are legally required to keep, for as long as that purpose lasts.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), and similar laws where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete your information,
  • stop or limit how we use it, and
  • give you a copy of it.

Email lottopatternph@prohubapps.com from the address on your Google account so we can confirm it is you. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

Delete your account

To delete your account and the personal information linked to it:

  • Send an email to lottopatternph@prohubapps.com with the subject “Delete my LottoPattern PH account”.
  • Use the email address of the Google account you sign in with.
  • We will confirm by reply and aim to finish within 30 days.

Deleting your account ends your trial and removes any unused credits. Please also remove the app from your device.

Security

Sign-in runs through Google, and your records are stored with Supabase, which protects data in transit and at rest. Access to your records is limited to your own account. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection.

Age limit

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

Where data is processed

Our service providers may process your information on servers outside the Philippines. By using the app you understand that your information may be handled in those places, under the protections these providers offer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your personal information, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies.
