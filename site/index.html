Patterns you can see and learn

Lotto Pattern PH organizes number relationships into understandable pattern types. Use the guide to learn how each pattern works, then explore combinations in the generator.

  • ✓Plus 2 — pairs such as 14 and 16.
  • ✓Twin numbers — repeated-digit numbers such as 11 or 22.
  • ✓Reversed pairs — pairs such as 14 and 41.
  • ✓Plus 10, 20, or 30 — number pairs with the corresponding difference.
